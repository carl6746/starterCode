There are two types of cameras:
    Perspective Camera * - Use linear perspective
    Orthographic Camera - Also known as a parallel projection

Domain of pixel coordinates:
    [-0.5,n_x-0.5]x[-0.5,n_y-0.5]
    
Aspect ratio: (Don't forget)
    width/height

What tmin and tmax should you choose for shadow rays?
    (tmin is 1,  tmax is INFINITY) -> From Eye
    (tmin is 0.0001, tmax = 1, d=lightPosition - ptShadow ) -> From Shadow
    1.0e^8 [smallest floating point value]
